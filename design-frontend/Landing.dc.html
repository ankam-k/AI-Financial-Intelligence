The product loop

Record → build history → analyze → discover → inspect evidence.

01 · Record
Expenses, check-ins, life events

Fast entry for spending, daily habits and the context surrounding them — travel, a new job, an exam period.

02 · Build history
One longitudinal timeline

Every record joins a single chronological history — the raw material any analysis depends on.

03 · Analyze
Statistical review, not guesswork

Once enough history exists, the analysis engine tests for relationships — it doesn't assume them.

04 · Understand
Insights with evidence attached

Every validated pattern links to the observations, sample size and statistical result behind it.

Evidence-first

Association, not advice.

An AI layer helps you ask questions in plain language — but it never invents a financial fact. It only renders what the analysis engine has already validated against your history.

Insights are only shown once your history clears the evidence threshold — never before.

Every finding links to its underlying observations, sample size and statistical result.

Language stays in the realm of association and recorded history — never causation or forecasting.

No demo accounts, no sample data — what you see is only what you've recorded.
